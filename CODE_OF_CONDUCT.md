# Code of conduct

Auxris is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Participants must communicate constructively, criticize ideas rather than people, respect privacy and media ownership, accept correction, and prioritize the safety of people affected by provenance claims. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate misrepresentation of evidence, doxxing, and publication of private media are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports should be sent privately through the repository's security-advisory channel when they contain sensitive information. The project owner is responsible for fair, proportionate enforcement and will avoid conflicts of interest where another maintainer can handle a report.

Enforcement actions may include a private warning, temporary restriction, removal from project spaces, or permanent ban depending on severity and pattern. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
