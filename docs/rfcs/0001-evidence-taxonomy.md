# RFC-0001: Evidence taxonomy and envelope

- Status: Proposed
- Target: v0.2
- Last updated: 2026-08-02

## Decision

Auxris is an evidence-producing trust layer for audio systems. It does not determine authorship, ownership, infringement, or whether audio was generated by AI. Every detector returns bounded observations in a common, versioned envelope; a separate policy evaluator converts those observations into operational decisions.

## Evidence classes

The first stable vocabulary is intentionally small:

- `recording_segment_match`: a time-aligned match against a recording in a catalog the operator controls.
- `speaker_similarity`: a calibrated similarity result from an optional speaker adapter. This is biometric evidence and is disabled by default.
- `watermark_detection`: a vendor-specific detector result with the vendor, detector version, and asserted payload.
- `content_credential`: a C2PA or equivalent manifest validation result, including trust-chain state.
- `operator_attestation`: a signed assertion supplied by an authorized organization.

Evidence is not interchangeable. A recording match cannot be presented as proof of speaker identity; absence of a watermark cannot be presented as proof that content is human-made.

## Envelope

`auxris.evidence.v1` contains:

1. immutable identifiers and creation time;
2. the scanned subject and its canonical SHA-256 digest;
3. detector identity, version, configuration digest, and execution mode;
4. typed findings with scores and detector-specific measurements;
5. provenance references supplied or discovered during the scan;
6. an explicit policy decision and reasons;
7. limitations and warnings;
8. an integrity block that can carry a detached signature in a future profile.

Scores are named and scoped. They are never represented as generic `confidence` unless the detector publishes a calibration definition and benchmark.

## Decision vocabulary

The policy layer emits `allow`, `review`, `quarantine`, `block`, or `not_evaluated`. A result must identify the policy bundle and revision used. Default open-source policy treats weak or ambiguous evidence as `review`, not `block`.

## Privacy and retention

The envelope must not contain raw audio, reusable voice embeddings, access tokens, or private catalog metadata. Operators set retention independently for media, embeddings, fingerprint landmarks, and evidence. Biometric adapters require purpose limitation, deletion support, access logging, and an operator-provided lawful basis.

## Compatibility

Consumers must ignore unknown optional fields but reject unknown major schema identifiers. New evidence classes can be added without changing the envelope major version. Removing or reinterpreting fields requires `auxris.evidence.v2`.

## Non-goals

- global copyright ownership resolution;
- legal advice or automatic takedown decisions;
- training-data membership inference from an output alone;
- universal synthetic-audio detection.
