# Design partners

Auxris is seeking teams with a real audio catalog and a concrete review decision. Good partners include AI-audio developers, sample or stock-media marketplaces, game and film studios, dataset maintainers, archives, and podcast networks.

## What we need

- 1,000–10,000 reference assets, or a smaller representative catalog for an initial evaluation.
- Known positive queries: excerpts, transcodes, noisy recordings, edits, or generated outputs that contain approved source material.
- Known negative queries that should abstain.
- Clear authorization to process every supplied fixture and an agreed retention policy.
- A human review decision that Auxris evidence could support.

## What a pilot produces

- A private, reproducible evaluation configuration.
- Recall, false-positive, abstention, latency, and index-size measurements.
- Examples of aligned evidence, including failures.
- A recommendation about whether the workflow merits integration.

We do not request that partners publish private audio. Public benchmark contributions must be owned by the contributor or redistributable under a documented license.

## Initial contact

Open a GitHub Discussion with no private information to express interest. Maintainers will arrange an appropriate private channel before any asset or contract details are shared.
